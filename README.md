# 🛰️ ASTRA — Astronaut Health Monitoring

An AI-assisted astronaut health monitoring and risk assessment prototype designed for long-duration space missions.

## 🚀 Overview

ASTRA provides a simple monitoring interface that combines simulated astronaut health indicators into an overall mission-health assessment.

The prototype helps visualize how changes in astronaut health measurements can affect the overall risk status during a space mission.

## ❤️ Key Features

- Real-time-style astronaut health dashboard
- Overall health score from 0–100
- Heart rate monitoring
- Blood pressure monitoring
- Body temperature monitoring
- Sleep-duration monitoring
- Interactive human health map
- 7-day health trend visualization
- Dynamic health-risk assessment
- Stable, Attention, and Critical status levels
- Simulated astronaut mission environment
- Responsive interface for mobile and desktop

## 🧠 Risk Assessment

ASTRA evaluates the simulated health inputs and generates a corresponding health status.

Example:

- **Stable** — indicators are within the simulated monitoring range
- **Attention** — one or more indicators require observation
- **Critical** — multiple indicators require immediate review

The dashboard also provides a recommended next action based on the assessment.

## 🛰️ Mission Context

The prototype is designed around the challenges of monitoring astronaut health during long-duration space missions, where continuous observation and early identification of potential health risks can support mission safety.

## 🛠️ Technology

## 📱 How to Use

1. Open the live demo.
2. Scroll to **ASTRA Quick Health Assessment**.
3. Enter simulated astronaut measurements.
4. Select the mission environment.
5. Tap **Analyze Health Status**.
6. Review the generated health score, risk level, trend, and recommended action.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This is a prototype created for demonstration and educational purposes.

All health measurements and assessment results are simulated and should not be interpreted as real medical advice or real astronaut medical data.
